Company Profile

Focus on Quality · A Vinyl Flooring Industry Benchmark

Dongyang Maosheng Plastic Co., Ltd. is an innovative enterprise with over 20‑year experience in plastic manufacturing. We specialize in the R&D, production, sales and import‑export business of PVC flooring.

Our product portfolio covers a full range of PVC flooring, including LVT, SPC, loose‑lay and self‑adhesive vinyl flooring.

We have obtained ISO9001 International Quality Management System Certification, ISO14001:2015 Environmental Management System Certification, AGBB, CE, 10P, SGS and US FLOORSCORE certifications.

With upgraded production facilities, we are equipped with 6 sets of hydraulic automatic production lines, 1 calendar roll machine, 3 UV coating & tempering lines, 4 multi‑functional cutting lines, 2 German‑made grooving lines, 1 vinyl floor extrusion production line and 5 SPC production lines. Our annual production capacity of PVC flooring reaches 7,200,000 square meters.

Located at No.15 Kexing Road, Hengdian Electronics Industrial Zone, Dongyang, Zhejiang Province — widely known as the “Oriental Hollywood”, our company occupies a total area of 33,000 m². Among this space, 26,000 m² houses standard workshops, R&D buildings and quality‑testing laboratories. We are fully equipped with complete inspection and testing instruments to satisfy diverse customer requirements.

Sincerely welcome customers from all over the world!

Our History

Milestones

For more than 20 years, Maosheng has focused on vinyl flooring manufacturing, building an industry reputation step by step.

2006

Founded

Dongyang Maosheng Plastic Co., Ltd. was established in Hengdian Industrial District, Dongyang, Zhejiang, starting its vinyl flooring journey.

2006-2014

Deep Development

Continuously optimizing production and product lines, gradually building the three series: Dryback, Click and Loose Lay.

2015

Multiple International Certifications

Products successively obtained ISO9001, ISO14001, CE, AGBB and US FLOORSCORE certifications, reaching international standards and laying a solid foundation for high-end overseas markets.

Aug 2015

Second Factory Completed

The second factory was completed and put into production, expanding the total area to 8.24 acres and greatly increasing daily capacity to meet global orders.

2015

Sales Exceeded $10M

Annual total sales reached USD 16 million, with products exported to Europe, the Americas, Africa and Southeast Asia.

2018-2019

International Exhibitions

Participated in Domotex Shanghai and SURFACES Las Vegas, continuously boosting the brand's international influence.

2026

Continuous Innovation

Upholding craftsmanship, deepening R&D and process upgrades, dedicated to providing better vinyl flooring solutions worldwide.

Industry Certifications
