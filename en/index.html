About Maosheng

Craft Manufacturing · A Globally Renowned Vinyl Flooring Supplier

With over 20 years of experience in plastic manufacturing, Maosheng specializes in the R&D, production, sales and import-export of PVC flooring, covering LVT, SPC, loose-lay and self-adhesive categories.

Certified to ISO9001, ISO14001:2015, CE, FLOORSCORE and other authoritative standards, our modern equipment cluster supports a stable annual capacity of 7,200,000 m².

Our 33,000 m² facility in Hengdian, Dongyang, Zhejiang serves customers in 30+ countries and regions worldwide.
